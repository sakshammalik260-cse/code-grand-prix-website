# Original cinematic artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for this redesign. The approved reference screenshots are visual guidance only; none are shipped as page backgrounds. Images contain no official F1/team logos or UI. The PNG originals remain in the imagegen output directory; the project uses visually equivalent compressed WebP copies.

## hero

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: cinematic website background plate, landscape 16:9, high resolution. Create a photorealistic premium motorsport night-race image. Camera extremely low from behind a realistic unbranded red open-wheel single-seater racing car, rear wing and broad slick tires prominent in LOWER LEFT foreground, car roughly occupies x10%-42%, y63%-100%. Red rear rain light glowing. Asphalt race circuit extends from bottom left across frame toward bright vanishing point at x60% y65%. Grandstands at left packed with indistinct spectators, red accent lighting; barriers and red horizontal light trails along right, brilliant floodlight towers midground right and distant track, subtle atmospheric haze. UPPER HALF predominantly deep near black sky with ample uncluttered dark space for overlaying a large white title in upper left-center and countdown card on right. Black #0b0b0f, saturated racing red #e10600, cold white lights. Photo-real commercial racing photography, convincing metallic reflections, no illustration/vector/cartoon. Image contains ONLY scenery, NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LOGOS, NO TEAM TRADEMARKS, NO F1 logo, NO UI, NO panels/buttons, no checkered flag; these are added in HTML. Landscape 16:9 composition. This is an original artwork.

## helmet

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape cinematic website background plate 16:9, high resolution. A photorealistic close-up portrait of an anonymous racing driver in a black and racing-red full-face helmet and black racing suit, dark visor concealing the face. The driver fills the LEFT 45% of the landscape image from top edge to bottom edge, cropped at shoulder/chest, head turned slightly toward right. Helmet has black carbon material, crisp red geometric stripes, dramatic red glowing edge lighting around visor, subtle brushed metal hardware and realistic fabric stitching. The helmet eye line in upper left half. RIGHT 55% is almost entirely smooth deep black negative space for actual HTML login controls. Very subtle dark red diagonal bands on far right edge only, restrained red haze in lower corners. Premium motorsport photography, deep shadows, realistic glossy helmet reflections and saturated red rimlight, black #0b0b0f and #e10600 red palette. NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO LOGOS, NO BADGES, NO UI, NO panels, NO F1/team trademarks. Do not put driver in center or right; left anchored dramatic close-up. Original artwork, 16:9 landscape.
